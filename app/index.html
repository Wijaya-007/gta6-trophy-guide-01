Roadmap to the Platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours (PowerPyx: difficulty 4/10). No online trophies, no mission replay, no difficulty settings — free-roam continues after the story. Keep cheats off: they disable trophies and saving.

    Missables & warnings

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story. Save manually at the start and end of every chapter — missions cannot be replayed.

    Sources

Trophy names and descriptions are the PlayStation / Xbox list as published on PSN and Xbox since the game launched, as collected by PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements. Rockstar has not published a separate official list, so anything not confirmed in two sources is marked unconfirmed.
